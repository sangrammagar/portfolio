About Me

Building practical AI systems through research, engineering, and hands-on development

I am an AI Developer and M.Tech graduate in Artificial Intelligence from Dr. B.R. Ambedkar NIT Jalandhar, with a B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering from PAHSU.
